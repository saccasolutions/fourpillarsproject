# LinkedIn Launch Post

84% of federal revenue comes from taxing human labor. What happens when AI replaces most of it?

I spent the last six months building the answer: a complete redesign of the American tax and safety net system. Full federal balance sheet. Monte Carlo validated. Open source.

The framework: eliminate income tax. Replace it with a 20% consumption tax (basics exempt) + 50% automation tax on AI labor savings. Fund a $25K guaranteed floor for every citizen. Replace Social Security with personal investment accounts you actually own.

Every income level comes out ahead. A minimum wage worker retires with $1M+ in their own account. The automation tax grows as AI grows — more displacement means more revenue.

I built this to be challenged. The whitepaper, 12 supporting documents, and 5 Python models are all open source.

If you're in economics, tax policy, or AI — I want to know what I got wrong.
