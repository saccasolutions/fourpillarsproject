# UBI Framework — Next Steps Roadmap (v4)
**Last updated:** April 18, 2026

---

## Completed

### Phase 1: Framework Design (v1)
- Framework design (4 pillars, floor, principles)
- 17 persona stress tests
- Economic Pillar GDP attribution research

### Phase 2: Core Redesign (v2)
- Pillar 1 formula redesign (GDP-linked market compensation)
- Tax structure redesign (consumption tax replacing income/SS tax)
- Retirement model (mandatory 401k + child seed accounts)
- Automation attribution correction (ghost salaries fund system)
- Eligibility defined (US citizens only)
- Incarceration policy defined (costs + trust + obligations)
- Dual-pillar attribution allowed
- Measurement simplified (existing data first, attestation for gaps)

### Phase 3: Federal Balance Sheet & Complete Model (v3)
- Complete federal balance sheet (all outflows + all revenue)
- Identified and fixed revenue gap from eliminated income/FICA taxes
- Phased floor design ($25K, full below $30K earnings, phases out by $80K)
- Healthcare model redesign (employer insurance stays, gov covers gaps, payroll penalty backstop)
- Employer FICA kept at 9%, wage cap removed
- Automation tax set at 50%
- Investment income tax at 25% (capital gains, dividends, interest)
- Corporate tax restored to 28%
- Financial transaction tax, corporate minimum tax, digital ad tax, buyback tax, estate tax reform
- Company vs individual revenue split analysis (37% / 57%)
- 10-year growth trajectory (balance by Year 7, surplus by Year 10)
- Inflation analysis (redistribution not money printing, 3-5% short-term, deflationary long-term)
- All personas updated to v3.0 with phased floor and VAT calculations
- Political brief (left/right/center messaging)
- Objections & responses (20 anticipated pushbacks with rebuttals)
- Transition plan (year-by-year implementation over 10 years)
- Executive summary (one-pager)

### Phase 4: Deep Economic Modeling & Whitepaper (v4)
- Pillar 1 calibration (BLS 22 occupation groups × BEA GDP, GDP Growth Factor analysis, weighted avg GF = 1.68)
- Tax revenue Monte Carlo simulation (2,000 iterations, 10-year projections, sensitivity analysis)
- Floor phase-out optimization (7 scenarios tested, current v3 confirmed optimal for launch)
- 401(k) transition design (20-year cash flow, cohort-based SS phase-out, individual outcome projections)
- Child seed account structure (4 scenarios, $5K seed + $1K/yr → $1.2M by age 65)
- Pillar 2-4 payment calibration (3-tier system for each, $549B total within $800B budget)
- Recession stress testing (6 scenarios: normal, 2008-type, COVID, stagflation, AI boom, stagnation)
- Automation displacement by occupation (22 groups ranked by risk and tax revenue potential)
- Pilot program design (75K participants, $2.5B/yr, 3-5 year RCT with comparison city)
- International comparison study (VAT implementations, UBI pilots, mandatory savings, NIT experiments)
- Expert engagement plan (18 named researchers, phased outreach strategy, tailored talking points)
- Full whitepaper compiled (peer-review format, 14 sections + appendices)
